# Wrap-up Checklist: {{project.name}}

## Delivery
- [ ] All deliverables handed over in the agreed form and location
- [ ] Acceptance confirmed in writing
- [ ] Source files, credentials and documentation transferred; client owns all accounts and repositories
- [ ] Short handover doc: how it works, how to deploy or edit, known limitations, next steps

## Access and security
- [ ] Remove my access to client systems; rotate any shared credentials
- [ ] Delete client data I no longer need, per what we agreed
- [ ] Confirm backups and monitoring are in the client's hands

## Money
- [ ] Final invoice sent and paid (or scheduled); outstanding expenses settled
- [ ] Records kept for tax and accounting

## Relationship
- [ ] Ask how it went and what could be better
- [ ] Ask for a testimonial or review, and permission to reference the project in a portfolio
- [ ] Offer a follow-up: maintenance, a phase 2, or a check-in in 30 days
