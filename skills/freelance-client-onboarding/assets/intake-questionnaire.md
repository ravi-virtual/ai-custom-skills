# Project Intake Questionnaire: {{project.name}}

Hi {{client.name}}, these answers let me plan accurately and avoid surprises. Short answers are fine; "not sure" is a valid answer.

## About the project
1. In one or two sentences, what are we trying to achieve, and why now?
2. How will you know the project succeeded? (a number, a behaviour, a launch)
3. Who are the users or audience?
4. Are there examples, competitors or references you like or dislike?

## Scope and constraints
5. Must-haves for the first version, and nice-to-haves for later?
6. Hard deadline or dependency (event, launch, contract)? What drives it?
7. Budget range you have in mind?
8. Anything that has been tried or decided already?

## Existing assets and access
9. What already exists (code, designs, content, brand guidelines, accounts)?
10. Which tools or systems are involved? Who can grant access?
11. Where should the work live (your repository, hosting, workspace)?

## People and process
12. Who is the decision maker, and who gives feedback? Is anyone else involved?
13. How and how often would you like updates?
14. Anything sensitive (personal data, regulated industry, NDA) I should know about?
15. Anything else I should have asked?
