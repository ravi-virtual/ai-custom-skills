# Reminder and Billing Message Templates

Edit to match your voice. Keep each message short, factual and easy to act on. Include the invoice number, amount, due date and how to pay.

## Sending an invoice

```
Subject: Invoice {number} for {project}: due {due_date}

Hi {client},

Attached is invoice {number} for {what it covers}: {currency} {amount}, due {due_date}.
Payment details and reference are on the invoice. Let me know if you need a PO number, a different billing contact or a different format.

Thanks,
{sender}
```

## Before the due date

```
Subject: Invoice {number} is due on {due_date}

Hi {client}, a quick heads-up that invoice {number} ({currency} {amount}) is due on {due_date}. Let me know if you need anything from my side to process it.
```

## Due today

```
Subject: Invoice {number} is due today

Hi {client}, invoice {number} ({currency} {amount}) is due today. If it is already on its way, thank you and please ignore this note.
```

## Gentle reminder (about 3 days late)

```
Subject: Reminder: invoice {number}

Hi {client}, I don't have a payment recorded for invoice {number} ({currency} {amount}, due {due_date}). It may have crossed with your payment; if not, could you confirm when to expect it? Happy to resend the invoice or payment details.
```

## Firm reminder (about 7 days late)

```
Subject: Overdue invoice {number}: payment needed by {deadline}

Hi {client}, invoice {number} ({currency} {amount}) is now {days} days past its due date. Please arrange payment by {deadline}, or tell me today if there is a problem with the invoice so we can resolve it.
```

## Final notice (about 14 days late)

```
Subject: Final notice: invoice {number}

Hi {client}, I haven't received payment or a reply about invoice {number} ({currency} {amount}, due {due_date}). If it isn't settled by {deadline}, I'll pause work on the project and apply the late-payment terms in our agreement. I'd much rather sort this out directly; can we speak this week?
```

## Formal notice (about 30 days late)

```
Subject: Formal notice: invoice {number} remains unpaid

Dear {client}, invoice {number} ({currency} {amount}) has been unpaid for {days} days despite earlier reminders. Please pay in full by {deadline}. Otherwise I will proceed with the next steps available under our agreement, which may include a formal demand, the platform's dispute process, or a collections or legal route.
```

Only state steps you are prepared and entitled to take. Collections and legal rules vary by country and by whether the client is a business or a consumer; take advice before using them.

## Dispute response

```
Subject: Re: invoice {number}

Hi {client}, thanks for flagging this. To resolve it quickly, could you tell me which line or amount you disagree with and why? I've attached the agreed scope and the work log for reference. I'm happy to talk it through on a short call [day/time]. If there is an error on my side, I'll correct it right away.
```

## Payment plan proposal

```
Subject: Payment plan for invoice {number}

Hi {client}, I understand cash flow can be tight. I can accept {currency} {amount_1} on {date_1} and {currency} {amount_2} on {date_2}, with the work resuming once the first payment arrives. If that works, please reply to confirm and I'll note it on the invoice.
```

## Thank-you receipt

```
Subject: Payment received: invoice {number}

Hi {client}, payment for invoice {number} has arrived. Thank you! A receipt is attached. It was a pleasure working with you; if you're happy with the result, a short review or testimonial would mean a lot.
```
