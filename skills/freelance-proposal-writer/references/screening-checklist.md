## Before bidding

- [ ] I can do this work well, and it is in a field where I have proof
- [ ] Budget or rate meets my minimum, or scope can be cut to fit it
- [ ] Deadline is realistic with my current workload
- [ ] The goal and deliverables are clear enough to price, or I can ask to clarify first
- [ ] No unpaid work, no off-platform requests, no equity-only pay
- [ ] The work is legal and complies with the platform's rules and the terms of any source I would use

## Client signals on the platform

Look at (where the platform shows them): payment method verified; total spent and hire rate; average rating and written feedback from freelancers; how many jobs were posted vs hired; whether the client replies and how they describe past contractors. A verified payment method with a history of paid, finished jobs is a stronger signal than a polished post. No history is not a red flag by itself; treat it as a reason to use milestones and clear scope.

## Questions that surface hidden scope

- What does "done" look like, and who approves it?
- Which systems, accounts or people do I depend on, and who gives access?
- Is there existing work (code, designs, content) I must fit into? Can I see it?
- What is the deadline driven by (launch, event, contract)?
- How many revision rounds are expected, and who gives feedback?
- What is the budget range you have in mind, so I propose the right scope?

## Polite decline messages

No fit:
```
Thanks for considering me. This is outside what I do best, and I'd rather point you to someone who can do it justice than overpromise. Good luck with the project.
```

Budget too low:
```
Thank you for the details. At that budget I can't deliver the scope described to the standard I hold. If it helps, I could do [reduced scope] for [price], or I'm happy to revisit if the budget changes.
```

Request for unpaid work or off-platform contact:
```
I keep paid work and communication on the platform so both of us are protected. I'm glad to do a short paid first milestone so you can see how I work.
```
